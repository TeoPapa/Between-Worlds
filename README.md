# BetweenWorlds

A 3D mixed perspective PC game, made in Unity Engine.

The story follows Noathyn as he ventures in the forests of the Kingom of Aleunr, as he tries to reach Xetniay's temple and stop the evil forces once and for all.

But Noathyn has a secret. He has a trait of being able to change between worlds. When he leaves Aleunr, he follows the footsteps of Anthony, a teenage boy struggling through adolescence.

This game was created as my University thesis. The game aims to inform the player for subjects such as: adolescent anxiety, learning difficulties, social difficulties and bullying. Throughout his journey, Anthony(/Noathyn) learns how to solve those issues (mostly through parallelism between the two worlds and explanations of different peers).
